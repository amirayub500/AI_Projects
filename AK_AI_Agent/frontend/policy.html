Demo Site Usage & Privacy Policy

Sample policy · Last updated October 2026

This is a sample template for a demonstration website. It is not legal advice. Have it reviewed before using it for a real service.

1. Purpose of this site

This site demonstrates the AK Data Solutions Research AI Agent. It is provided for evaluation and testing only and is not a production service.

2. Sample data

Questions that start with / are answered from a fictional demo dataset (donors, companies, pledges and documents). All people, organizations and amounts in it are made up. Any resemblance to real persons or organizations is coincidental.

3. What you should not enter

  • Personal information about yourself or others (names, addresses, ID numbers, health data).
  • Confidential, financial or business-sensitive information.
  • Passwords, keys or other credentials.

4. What we collect

  • The questions you submit and the answers generated.
  • Technical data needed to run the service, such as IP address and request time, used for rate limiting and troubleshooting.

Requests may appear in server logs. Logs are used only to operate and improve the demo, are not sold or shared for marketing, and are deleted periodically.

5. Web research

Questions without / trigger searches on the public web. Your question text is sent to a search provider and web pages are downloaded to answer it.

6. Accuracy

Answers are generated by an AI model and may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Check important information against the cited sources. Nothing on this site is financial, legal or professional advice.

7. Acceptable use

Do not use the demo for unlawful purposes, to attempt to access systems or data you are not authorized to access, or to overload the service. Usage limits apply, and access may be restricted or withdrawn at any time.

8. Availability

The demo may be offline, slow or changed without notice. Its web address may change.

9. Contact

Questions about this demo or this policy: AK Data Solutions, via the contact details on our website.
